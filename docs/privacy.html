Privacy Policy

Tune-AL · last updated 16 November 2026

In short

Tune-AL does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Microphone

Tune-AL asks for microphone access only to hear your instrument and detect its pitch. The sound is analysed in real time on your device and immediately discarded. It is never recorded, saved, or sent over the internet.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few settings locally on your device, such as your chosen reference pitch (A4), appearance (light/dark) and whether you have seen the welcome screen. These never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

Tune-AL contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, and makes no network connections.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it is safe to use for people of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
